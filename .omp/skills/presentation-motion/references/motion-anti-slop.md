# Anti-motion-slop

Reject motion that is only visual noise:

- a fade-up applied to every element;
- random or all-element stagger;
- bounce, elastic overshoot, spin, float, pulsing glow, decorative looping, random blur, and meaning-free zoom;
- continuous parallax or 3D camera transitions;
- character-by-character entrances, typewriter text, or exploding letters without a content reason;
- a slide transition that hides the message more than it clarifies the transition.

Ask two questions before retaining an effect:

1. Does it clarify information hierarchy?
2. Does it explain sequence, causality, comparison, containment, or handoff?

If both answers are no, remove it. Do not add ambient motion merely to make a static slide feel busy. Match motion weight to the deck’s typography, color restraint, whitespace, and image treatment in `DESIGN_DNA.md`.
